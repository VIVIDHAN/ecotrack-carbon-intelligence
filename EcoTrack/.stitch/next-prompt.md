A desktop dashboard overview page for EcoTrack, a personal carbon footprint tracker.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
* Base: Warm off-white / very light neutral background (#F6F7F2)
* Surface: White (#FFFFFF)
* Primary Green: Soft green (#2F7D4A)
* Dark Green: Deep charcoal/green (#17452B)
* Mint: (#DFF3E5)
* Teal: (#2A9D8F)
* Amber: (#F2B84B) for achievements
* Text: Deep charcoal (#172019)
* Muted Text: (#69736B)
* Border: (#E4E8E3)
* Red/orange only for warnings

Visual Personality: "Editorial sustainability dashboard + premium fintech analytics + playful habit tracker". Think "Apple Health x Duolingo x modern climate analytics". Use rounded corners, ample whitespace, and typography that conveys a serious but approachable feeling.

**Page Structure:**
1. Sidebar navigation with items: Overview, My Footprint, Daily Habits, Challenges, Insights, Achievements, Settings, Help.
2. Top Header with: Search, Notification Bell (🔔), and User Profile.
3. Main Content Area:
   - Greeting: "Good morning, Alex 👋 Small actions. Measurable impact."
   - Hero Section ("Your Carbon Footprint"): Show "4.8 kg CO₂ today", with a trend "↓ 12% vs your average", and a benchmark "Your average 5.4 kg", "Target 4.2 kg". Include a visual circular progress indicator.
   - Footprint DNA Breakdown: A simple bar/DNA visualization showing percentages: Transport (38%), Energy (24%), Food (18%), Shopping (12%), Other (8%). Do NOT use a pie chart.
   - Today's Missions section: Cards for daily habits with XP rewards (e.g., "🚌 Take public transit, Avoid ~1.4 kg CO₂, +40 XP").
   - A streak section showing a 7-day streak with "🔥 7-day streak" and circles for days of the week.
