# Design System

## Overall Visual Direction
* Base: Warm off-white / very light neutral background (#F6F7F2)
* Surface: White (#FFFFFF)
* Primary Green: Soft green (#2F7D4A)
* Dark Green: Deep charcoal/green (#17452B)
* Mint: (#DFF3E5)
* Teal: (#2A9D8F)
* Amber: (#F2B84B) for achievements
* Text: Deep charcoal (#172019)
* Muted Text: (#69736B)
* Border: (#E4E8E3)
* Red/orange only for warnings

## Visual Personality
"Editorial sustainability dashboard + premium fintech analytics + playful habit tracker"
Think: "Apple Health x Duolingo x modern climate analytics"
Not an environmental NGO website.

## UX Principles
1. How am I doing? (Carbon score)
2. What caused my footprint? (Footprint breakdown)
3. What can I do today? (Today's eco actions)
4. Am I improving? (Trends + achievements)

Use rounded corners, ample whitespace, and typography that conveys a serious but approachable feeling.
